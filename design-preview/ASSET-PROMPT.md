# Visuel de l’étude Anatomie

Génération native d’image, fond transparent. Asset utilisé : `assets/pizza-layers.png`. Composition illustrative, aucune recette officielle revendiquée.

## Prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: production-quality transparent texture atlas for an interactive exploded-view pizza on a premium restaurant website. This is ONE square image divided into an invisible precise 2 by 2 grid of equal square cells. No borders. Each cell contains ONLY ONE ingredient layer, photographed with exactly the same fixed camera, scale, perspective, center alignment and lighting, so the four cells can be overlaid to assemble a realistic margherita pizza.
Camera: 40 degrees above horizontal, orthographic product photography; a round pizza appears as a wide ellipse with height about 60% of its width. Each layer centered in its own cell at precisely the midpoint. Pizza full outer width about 86% of cell width. Transparent alpha background, no ground plane, no plate, no cast shadows outside ingredients.
TOP LEFT cell: only a thin authentic Italian baked pizza dough base with a modest airy blistered golden rim, naturally mottled toasted brown and a few char marks, empty center, no sauce or toppings. Beautiful artisanal texture, not a bagel, not thick bread.
TOP RIGHT cell: only a thin circular irregular-edged layer of glossy cooked red tomato sauce matching the INNER area of that same pizza (about 72% of cell width), no dough, no rim, no cheese. A natural thin sauce surface with delicate texture, not a bowl or thick solid object.
BOTTOM LEFT cell: only 9 to 12 irregular softly melted ivory mozzarella patches, with a few pale golden toasted spots, arranged across an elliptical pizza footprint of about 70% of cell width. Transparent gaps between patches. No dough, no sauce, no plate.
BOTTOM RIGHT cell: only 7 fresh small green basil leaves distributed naturally across that same elliptical footprint (about 66% of cell width), some individual, one small cluster. Transparent between leaves. No cheese, no sauce, no base.
Ultra-realistic high-end food advertising photography. Soft directional warm studio key light from upper left, controlled appetizing highlights, tactile material detail, restrained natural color. All four ingredients must fit entirely within their cell. Nothing crosses cell boundaries. No text, labels, letters, numbers, watermark, logo, garnish outside the four specified layers. EXACTLY four cells and each component shown only once. The empty space must be genuinely transparent, not gray checkerboard baked into image.
